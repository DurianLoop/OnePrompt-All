\begin{abstract}
AdaPromptSet studies reference-guided prompt-set selection with frozen matching and segmentation modules. The current MVP evidence has reached a Gate-F pivot: the MoNuSAC oracle gap is below the predeclared threshold after the one allowed rescue bank. This paper therefore reports the oracle-analysis state of the project: a fixed structured action is close to the per-query oracle under the evaluated bank, representation diagnostics do not justify MoNuSAC policy training, and external-domain evidence remains predeclared but insufficient for a new formal claim. All numbers are generated from local artifacts and the text is bounded to the current oracle-analysis-only claim.
\end{abstract}
